# WeightSumMore support

Contact the developer by [opening a WeightSumMore support issue](https://github.com/Cold-ay/weight-sum-more/issues/new). Include your app version, device model and the steps that led to the issue. This is a public forum. Please leave out personal measurements, health records and screenshots containing private information.

## Start a guided workout

Open More → Saved routines. Save a sample routine or create one by selecting exercises and durations. In Weekly planner, add the routine to today. Open Today and tap the start button on the training buddy card. Each move has a timer. Pause, resume or finish early, then use the rest timer between moves.

## Import an Apple Health workout

Open More → Import from Health, allow read access and select the workouts you want to add. On iPhone, you can review these permissions in Health. Manual workout entry works independently of Apple Health.

## Change language or units

Open More → Profile to choose English or Traditional Chinese and change your measurement units.
