# WeightSumMore privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026

WeightSumMore is a workout, meal and weight journal for iPhone and iPad. The developer does not receive your journal entries or use them for advertising, tracking or analytics.

## Information on your device

The app stores your profile, measurements, workouts, meal entries, goals and training plans in its local database. It uses these entries to show your history, run training timers and calculate estimates. Your language, unit and timer preferences are also saved on the device. Device backups are managed through your Apple settings.

## Apple Health and notifications

If you choose to import workouts, WeightSumMore asks permission to read workouts and active energy from Apple Health. You select the workouts to copy into your journal. The app does not write to Apple Health. You can change its access in Health or Settings. Revoking access stops future reads. You can remove already imported entries from the training log.

If you enable rest reminders, your device schedules the notifications locally.

## Your choices and support

You can edit or delete individual log entries in the app. Deleting the app and its data removes its local database. Apple Health originals and device backups are managed separately. External reference links open websites operated by their respective publishers.

For questions about your privacy, contact the developer through [WeightSumMore support](https://github.com/Cold-ay/weight-sum-more/blob/app-store/support/SUPPORT.md). The support issue tracker is public. Please leave personal measurements, workout histories and other health information out of your message.
